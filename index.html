About Me

I'm a Singapore Polytechnic student with a growing interest in cybersecurity, particularly penetration testing. My interest started through hands-on IT work — setting up hardware, resetting network switches, and troubleshooting systems during my internship — which sparked curiosity about how those same systems can be attacked and defended. Alongside my coursework in networking, Windows security and backend development, I explore offensive security through hands-on labs, and I'm looking for a role where I can keep learning under good mentorship.

Why Cybersecurity

I'm drawn to the problem-solving side of security: understanding how systems work well enough to see how they can fail, and helping organisations fix weaknesses before real attackers find them. I'm still early in my career, and I see that as a reason to seek mentorship and keep building fundamentals — networking, operating systems and web technologies — rather than a reason to hold back.

Problem-Solving Example

Debugging AV equipment under time pressure

AV Class Representative · Geylang Methodist School
  • Situation: As class AV rep, I was responsible for the projector, visualiser, and computer setup for every lesson. On several occasions, equipment failed to connect minutes before a teacher needed it.
  • Task: Get the system working before the lesson started, without disrupting the class or relying on outside IT support.
  • Action: I worked through the problem methodically — checking cable connections, restarting the display source, and testing alternate input ports — rather than guessing at fixes. Over time I learned the equipment's common failure points and could diagnose issues faster.
  • Result: Lessons consistently started on time, and teachers came to rely on me as the go-to person for AV issues. It taught me to stay calm and work through a technical problem systematically under pressure — the same mindset I'd bring to diagnosing a vulnerability during an engagement.
